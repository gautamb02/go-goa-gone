It's a long weekend. Friday is Gandhi Jayanti. The south stays calm; the north fills up on Saturday and Sunday, so the north days stay around Morjim, Ashwem and Arambol.

Friday is a dry day. No alcohol is sold anywhere on 2 October, shacks included. Bars may stop serving at midnight on Thursday, so ask.

The monsoon is just leaving. Expect humidity and the odd evening shower. Rain covers, grippy sandals for Cola and Arambol, and a dry bag for the boat.

Scooters. Two automatics, yellow and black plates only. Film a walk-around at pickup, fill up straight away, and get helmets for rider and pillion.

Nobody rides after drinking. Thursday's bars are a walk from Priyanka's. Saturday is cabs both ways. On Sunday, nobody rides until they've slept.

For Stomp. 21+ government ID (DigiLocker or physical), smart-casual clothes and closed shoes. Ask Stomp Media whether 9 pm is a hard entry cutoff.

If you have energy left

Quiet spots that fit around the plan. All have few reviews, which usually means few people.

Colomb sunset pointSouth

A small hike from Palolem's south end to rocks and a hill view. One reviewer spotted dolphins. Wear shoes: there's broken glass on the sand.

Left out, and why

Kakolem and Cabo de Rama would add another 30 km of riding on Friday on top of Cola. Keri, Tiracol and the Arambol banyan tree mean more riding and an uphill trail on hangover day. Leopard Valley has mixed reviews, and one reviewer paid a ₹900 entry that wasn't redeemable. Anand in Vagator has nearly 9,800 reviews and hour-long waits, a bad bet before a 9 pm entry.
